QUILL HARBOR RECORD — SYNTHETIC BENCHMARK TEXT, CYCLE ONE

The morning ledger opens with the usual tallies of rope, pitch, and
lamp oil. Nothing in this record refers to any real place or person;
every name below exists only inside the Plume benchmark fixtures.

Entry the first: the customs shed reports twelve crates of dyed wool,
four crates of tin whistles, and one crate marked only with a spiral.
The spiral crate is to be held until the assessor returns from the
northern quay.

Entry the second: the harbor master of record for this cycle is
Odalric Fenn, confirmed by the seal registry at the top of the page.
Visiting captains should direct berth disputes to the master's office
before the noon bell.

Entry the third: Captain Mirelda Voss of the ketch "Saltwren" filed a
protest about gull nests in the crane rigging. The protest is noted
and deferred. Captain Voss is not the harbor master, whatever the
dockhands say about who really runs the quay.

Entry the fourth: the running ledger total for the season stands at
417 barrels, counted twice by the assessor's clerk and once by the
night watch, all three counts agreeing.

Entry the fifth: the lamp at the mole was trimmed at dusk. The keeper
notes that the wick supply will last another two cycles at the current
rate, three if the fog holds off.

End of record. The clerk's mark is affixed below the final line.
